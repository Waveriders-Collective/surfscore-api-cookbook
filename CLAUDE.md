## Maintaining this repo: it is public

Everything here, including commit messages, pull request titles and descriptions, and
issue comments, is readable by anyone. Before every commit and every PR:

1. **No organisation or customer data.** No org, customer or partner names. No ids
   (session, map, device, H3 cell, org, user). No row counts, percentages or other
   figures measured from anyone's data, including our own test org. Use placeholders
   such as `<session-id>`.
2. **Test results are counts only.** Report `tests/smoke.sh` as "N passed, N failed,
   N warnings, N skipped". Never paste its output: it contains ids and data figures.
3. **No secrets.** Keys live in `.env` (gitignored); `.env.example` holds placeholders only.
4. **No internal references.** No links or `#numbers` pointing at private repositories,
   issues or documents. Describe the change itself instead.
5. **No AI attribution.** No co-author trailers or session links for AI tools, no
   "generated by" lines, no tool or model names. Commits are authored by the maintainer.
6. Run `tests/check_public.sh` and fix everything it reports before committing.
